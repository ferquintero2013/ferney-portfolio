    Document validation with Vision AI

    Live

Background

The unusual part of my profile isn't the AI. It's the thirteen years before it.

I wire marketing, sales and operations processes together with n8n, Zapier, Python and LLMs. The reason I get called back isn't the tooling.

I started as a developer in Java and Scala, led QA on one of the largest projects in the recent history of the Colombian Stock Exchange, then spent years as a project manager, Scrum Master and Agile Coach at Nequi, Sophos, Imagemaker and Softgic.

That path means I sat on the business side of the conversation for a long time — mapping processes, negotiating scope, and watching automation projects fail. They rarely failed because the tooling was hard. They failed because nobody modeled the process properly before building on top of it.

So I design for the failure cases, not just the happy path. Error handling, retries, escalation to a human and logging go in from the start, because an automation that fails silently is worse than no automation at all. And I document what I build: if I'm the only person who understands it, I didn't finish the job.
